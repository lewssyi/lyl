# Final XAI Positioning

**The XAI component is a guarded post-hoc analysis framework rather than a
validated superior attribution algorithm.**

**XAI模块是一个具有严格审计和可靠性边界的事后分析框架，而不是已经验证优于传统LIME的新归因算法。**

The main paper innovation is the A1-zero dual-view sequence sleep-staging model
and its classification mechanism. XAI supports auxiliary interpretation,
failure-mode analysis, and separate clinical-semantic annotation. It is not a
performance-improvement module, a training mechanism, or a core superiority
algorithm. The sole canonical method is Context-Preserving Classic
Sequence-LIME, described only as exploratory Local Perturbation Sensitivity.

The paper may claim a reproducible context-preserving query pipeline, strict
parity and memory guards, a reliability taxonomy, protocol-consistent negative
results, deterministic case selection, and an implementation-valid separated
CCA layer. It may not claim model-faithful, validated, causal, clinically
validated, or superior attribution; it may not imply that explanation feedback
improved the classifier.
